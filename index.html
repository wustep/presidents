The game travels under a lot of names — Presidents, Scum, Asshole, Capitalism, Bum, Landlord — and nearly all of them descend from the Japanese climbing game Daifugō (大富豪), which is why the sharpest mechanics here still carry their Japanese names. Climbing games share a spine: shed your hand, match the count, beat the rank. Almost every variant is an argument about how easily you should be able to escape the hierarchy that the exchange keeps rebuilding.
